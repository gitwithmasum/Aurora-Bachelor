02 / HOW IT WORKS

Separate the records.
Clarify the balance.

Aurora keeps meal accounting and house accounting as distinct tracks. At settlement time, the relevant figures are brought together so members can review their monthly positions.

  • Record meals and meal-related costs.
  • Track shared house bills and member payments.
  • Review reports and settlement figures before acting.
  • Coordinate weekly household responsibilities.
